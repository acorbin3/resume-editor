Why I built this

I'm an engineering manager. I've read thousands of resumes and watched good people get filtered out for reasons that had nothing to do with their work.

1

The parser sees it first

Two columns, icons, text boxes, or a second job title without the company next to it, and the system fills in the wrong fields or none. The recruiter sees a mess and moves on.

2

Builders all look the same

Sidebars and skill bars parse badly, and Word templates drift until last month's one page is a page and a half.

3

Tailoring works, and nobody does it

Matching the posting's wording where it's true is what gets replies. By hand it's an hour per application, so it gets skipped.

What you get

✓

Your content in the plain template engineers and recruiters already know. Download the PDF or the LaTeX.

✓

ATS check with fixes

Runs on the actual PDF: text, reading order, contact info, headings, dates, and whether every role names its employer. Free, no limit.

✓

Tailor to a job

Paste the posting or a link. See the keyword match, then accept, edit, or skip each rewording. It won't claim anything your resume doesn't back up.

Every change is a diff you approve. Every version is kept. Highlight text on the PDF to ask for a change, or edit the LaTeX yourself.
