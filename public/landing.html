Why this exists

Three things quietly cost people interviews, and the usual resume tools make them worse.

1

Most rejections happen before a human looks

Applicant tracking systems turn your PDF into fields. Two-column layouts, icons, text boxes, skill bars, and a second title without the company on its own line get scrambled or dropped. The recruiter sees a mess, or nothing, and moves on.

2

Builders make generic resumes

Colored sidebars and star ratings look the same to every recruiter and parse badly. Word templates drift every time the text reflows, so the one-page version you sent last month is now a page and a half.

3

Tailoring is what works, and nobody has time

Rewording bullets to match the language of each posting is the single biggest lever on response rate. Done by hand it takes an hour per application, so it gets skipped.

What happens to your resume

Your resume is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to do the rebuilding and editing, and nowhere else. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API data is not used to train models.
Only you can see your resumes. Delete any of them whenever you like.
Claude is told to never invent employers, dates, numbers, or skills. If a posting asks for something you don't have, it says so instead of making it up.
